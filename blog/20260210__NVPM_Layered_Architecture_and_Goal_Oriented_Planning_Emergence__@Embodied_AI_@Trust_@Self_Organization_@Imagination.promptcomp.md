- A4. Continuous background imagination serves as threat/opportunity detector and precursor to curiosity, enabling adaptive scanning without explicit goals.

- A7. Bootstrap/instinctual layer is a thin, evolvable NN that provides homeostasis but must be overwritten by self-organization to avoid expert-graph rigidity.
